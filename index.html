About Chang Yung Metal Work

Based in Taichung, Taiwan, Chang Yung Metal Work Co., Ltd. has been manufacturing high-quality saw blades for over three decades. Our brand M-Specialist represents our commitment to precision cutting tools that serve professionals and hobbyists worldwide.

We specialize in stamping, heat treatment, and edge grinding processes — the core technologies behind every high-performance blade. Whether you need standard products or custom OEM specifications, our in-house capabilities cover the entire production chain from raw material to finished, packaged product.
